Experiment 01

Less time detail.

Play the demo and lower the sample rate to 8,000 Hz. Listen as the bright sweep loses its top end. The highest representable frequency is half the sample rate.

Experiment 02

Less level detail.

Keep the sample rate high, then lower the resolution to 4 bits. There are only 16 amplitude levels. Listen for the roughness, especially as notes fade.

Experiment 03

Your voice, digitized.

Record a sentence, then stop recording and press play. Compare Original and Adjusted. Files and recordings stay in this browser; audio is mixed to mono, up to 60 seconds.
